## Optional "Eneba catalog" mode

When a visitor turns on the "Eneba catalog" switch, product titles, prices and cover images come from a one-off snapshot of public eneba.com listing pages, shown for demonstration to the Eneba interview team. Cover images are loaded directly from Eneba's image CDN (imgproxy.eneba.games) and remain the property of their publishers and Eneba; they are not stored in this repository. This mode is off by default.
